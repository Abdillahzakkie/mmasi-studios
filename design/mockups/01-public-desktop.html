MmasiEjikeme Studios — Public site contact sheet

The public website drawn on a 1440×900 laptop. The scrolling pages are .webframes — drawn whole, header to footer — and the three viewport-locked states (an open lightbox, the video player, a shared link) are fixed .desk frames that sit below the persistent header.

The work leads. Every photograph and reel is a .plate — a graded gradient with grain and a vignette — so a frame reads as an image without shipping a single binary. The homepage and galleries lead in the cinematic .dark; pub-services and pub-about are drawn in the editorial light to show the theme toggle both sites carry.
Every path to booking is one tap to WhatsApp. The green .btn.wa sits in the header, on the hero, in the lightbox, in the player and on every service. The number is +234 814 709 9175. pub-contact shows the inquiry builder beside the exact message it will send, so nothing is retyped.
Content is authored 1:1 inside each frame; the sheet scales the whole frame with the zoom property, never the outline alone. Every class comes from system.css.
